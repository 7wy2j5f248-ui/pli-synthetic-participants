# Persona structure reference

The uploaded Persona Bank is a structural prototype only. Its cases are not a source pool.

## Compact hidden card

Create each participant from this shape:

> **[private ID]** — [adult age or age band], [birth/current location and optional migration context]. [occupation or daily role and schedule]. [household/living arrangement]. [usual sleep timing and continuity]. [one or two distinctive influences, habits, constraints, or beliefs].

The prototype demonstrates that a short card can combine:

- age and life stage;
- current setting and, where relevant, migration context;
- occupation, daily responsibilities, or shift pattern;
- household or living arrangement;
- usual sleep timing, duration, regularity, and interruptions;
- relevant evening or morning behavior;
- a personal concern, belief, environmental influence, or coping habit.

## Private depth for consistent answers

Before opening PLI, privately decide a few details that may emerge naturally:

- a typical weekday and weekend;
- the most recent night's approximate timeline;
- what the participant thinks is normal or problematic;
- factors that improve or worsen sleep;
- effects on mood, attention, work, or family life;
- what they have tried and how certain they feel;
- vocabulary, formality, and answer length appropriate to the assigned language and context.

These details are simulation controls, not a script. Reveal them only when the interviewer elicits them.

## Independence and coverage check

Reject and regenerate a proposed persona if it copies a prototype name, is a translation of a prototype case, or preserves a prototype's distinctive bundle of age, work, household, migration, and sleep traits.

Across a run, seek plausible variation rather than demographic quotas unless the manifest specifies quotas. Avoid making nationality, ethnicity, gender, age, or occupation a shortcut for sleep behavior. Each participant should feel individually coherent, culturally plausible, and different from earlier participants.
